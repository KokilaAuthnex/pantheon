Privacy Policy for Pantheon

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

Pantheon (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Authnex Technologies (Private) Limited (“Authnex”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains how Pantheon handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Pantheon is designed with privacy and security in mind.

Authentication Data

The App stores authentication secrets and account information locally on your device to generate One-Time Passwords (OTP/TOTP).

This may include:

  • Account names
  • Issuer/service names
  • TOTP secret keys
  • Configuration data required for authentication

These secrets are stored securely on your device and are not shared with third parties unless explicitly stated by the service you are using.

2. Camera Permission

Pantheon may request access to your device camera to:

  • Scan QR codes for account setup
  • Configure TOTP authentication

Camera data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Internet Access

Pantheon may use internet connectivity for:

  • Authnex-integrated authentication services
  • Secure communication with supported platforms
  • App updates and service improvements

Standard TOTP code generation works offline and does not require internet access.

4. Data Storage

Authentication data is primarily stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally collect or store:

  • Your passwords
  • Banking information
  • Personal messages
  • OTP verification codes generated by the App

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and backups.

5. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information, including:

  • Secure local storage practices
  • Encrypted communication where applicable
  • Protection against unauthorized access

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

Pantheon may integrate with third-party platforms or services that support Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Pantheon may collect limited anonymous technical information to improve app stability and performance, such as:

  • Crash reports
  • Device model information
  • App version
  • Diagnostic logs

This information does not intentionally identify individual users.

8. Children’s Privacy

Pantheon is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your device secure
  • Protecting your authentication codes
  • Maintaining backup or recovery methods when supported

Loss of your device may result in loss of access to your authentication accounts.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or through updated publication channels.
