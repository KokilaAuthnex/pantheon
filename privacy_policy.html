Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes the data practices for the Pantheon Authenticator application. Our core philosophy is that your security data belongs to you and should remain under your control.

2. Information Collection and Usage

No Personal Identification: We do not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

To provide its core functionality, the App interacts with your device in the following ways:

  • Camera Access: The App requires access to your device camera solely to scan QR codes provided by third-party services to set up TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) accounts. Images from the camera are processed locally on your device and are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Local Storage: All account secrets and generated codes are stored locally on your device. We do not host any server-side databases for your credentials, nor do we share this data with third parties.
  • Usage Details: We may collect non-identifying technical information regarding your device type and operating system version to ensure app compatibility and performance.

3. Data Security

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, the security of your information relies on your device's own security measures (such as biometrics, passcodes, or encryption). We recommend using built-in device security features to prevent unauthorized physical access to your authenticator codes.

4. Third-Party Services

This App generates codes for third-party services you choose to add. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party services. Please consult the privacy policies of the specific services (e.g., Google, GitHub, etc.) for which you are using Pantheon Authenticator.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we have no personal data to disclose to third parties, law enforcement, or advertising networks.

6. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

It is our policy to post any changes we make to our privacy policy on this page. If we make material changes, we will notify you through a notice within the App.

7. Contact Information

To ask questions or comment about this privacy policy and our privacy practices, contact us via the app support channels.
